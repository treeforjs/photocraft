# Feature coverage and remaining work

Menu coverage measures whether commands are wired, rather than fidelity or completed professional workflows. Use the generated [menu inventory](parity.md), [scorecard](scorecard.md) and [roadmap](roadmap.md) for current measurements and known gaps.

The previous numerical estimate and hour projections were not backed by reproducible workflow tests. They have been withdrawn. Remaining work includes document rendering fidelity, selection quality, typography, raw-development depth, platform printing and compatible plug-in workflows. A working menu entry alone does not establish that these workflows are complete.

Before claiming parity in an area, keep a reproducible input, expected output, platform and test command. Include performance and round-trip results where they affect normal use. Report unsupported behavior directly instead of treating a heuristic approximation as equivalent.
